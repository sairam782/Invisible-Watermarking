Paper (5 pages, 2 figures, 3 tables):

  Title: Distribution-Preserving QIM: Trading Noise Margin for Statistical
         Undetectability and Scaling Robustness in Invisible Watermarking

  Summary: QIM watermarks leave a lattice fingerprint in DCT coefficient
  histograms that removal attacks such as UnMarker exploit. We study a QIM
  variant that keeps the coefficient distribution natural and compare it
  with classical QIM at matched PSNR on real photographs, with
  source-grouped cross-validation and bootstrap confidence intervals. It is
  close to undetectable (learned-detector AUC 0.52 vs 0.90 at 64 bits) and
  gains 20-35 percentage points under blur, downscaling and contrast
  scaling, but loses 14-28 points under JPEG and noise and gives no
  advantage against UnMarker-style removal.
